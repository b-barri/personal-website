# News (Pull Based Model)

**A news feed for Indian markets that waits for you to ask, instead of buzzing all day.**

Most market apps are built to interrupt. Something ticks up two percent and your phone lights up, and by lunch you've got forty notifications and no idea which one actually mattered. I wanted the opposite: a feed I could open when I had a minute, that told me what moved and why, then got out of the way.

So this is a [[pull-based::you go and fetch the news when you want it, instead of the app pushing alerts at you. Like checking the fridge versus a waiter hovering at your table]] model. It surfaces the stocks that actually moved today across Indian markets, plus the business stories that are trending, and pins a bit of context to each one so a price jump comes with a reason and not just a red or green number. The whole point is signal over noise, fewer stories, each one worth the read.
